Privacy Policy - petgo

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information in the petgo app (available at petgo.faishal.id) and on this web page.

1. Data Collection

The petgo app is designed as an offline-first, private app. We do not collect, store on any server, track, transmit, or share your personal data with any party. No accounts, no registration, and no behavioral tracking inside the app.

2. Local Storage

All of your records, progress, and settings are stored locally on your device (browser local storage or the device's local database). This data is entirely under your control and is deleted when you delete the app's data or clear your browser storage cache.

3. Third-Party Services

Inside the app: we use no third-party analytics SDKs, show no tracking ads, and use no external service that records your activity. This web page (landing page): served from a static server, it loads fonts from Google Fonts and flag images from flagcdn.com; both services may log your IP address in their standard server logs when your browser loads those resources. Apart from that, this page loads no other trackers.

4. Cookies

This web page and the petgo app set no cookies of our own and use no cookie-based tracking. Note that Google Fonts and flagcdn.com may apply their own storage or caching policies when your browser requests resources from them; that is beyond our control.

5. Lawful Basis (GDPR Article 6)

The lawful basis for processing under Article 6 GDPR is (a) performance of the service you requested (providing the app) and (b) our legitimate interest in providing a private, offline-first app. We do not sell your personal data — there is no data we collect to sell.

6. Data Retention

Because all data stays only on your device, there is no retention period on our side. Your data is deleted when you remove the app, delete its data, or clear your browser storage cache.

7. Your Rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection over your personal data under the GDPR. Since we store nothing on our servers, there is no server-side data we can provide or delete; for any question, contact support@faishal.id and we will help.

8. Children

petgo is designed for family use, including children with parent or guardian supervision. Because the app works fully offline and we collect no data at all, no children's data is collected, stored on any server, or shared. Parents or guardians hold full control over the device and the data on it.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Every change is published on this page together with the latest version date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@faishal.id.
