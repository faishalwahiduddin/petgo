Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information in the PetGo app (available at petgo.faishal.id).

1. Data Collection

We value your privacy. The PetGo app is designed as a fully offline-first, private app. We do not collect, store on any server, track, transmit, or share your personal data with any third party. Everything you enter stays on your device.

2. Local Storage

All your records, checklists, and settings are stored locally on your device (browser local storage or the app's local database). This data is fully under your control and is deleted when you clear the app data or browser local-storage cache.

3. Third-Party Services

To be transparent: inside the app there are no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking ads, and no trackers of any kind. However, this web landing page loads fonts from Google Fonts and flag images from flagcdn.com; both services may log your IP address when the page loads — this is beyond our control. We do not add any tracking pixels or analytics scripts of our own to this page.

4. Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies on this page and use no cookies for analytics or advertising. The third-party CDNs above may apply their own technical mechanisms under their own policies.

5. Lawful Basis

To the extent data-protection rules (including GDPR Article 6 and Indonesia's PDP Law) apply: because we process no personal data on any server, there is no server-side processing requiring a lawful basis on our part. Any processing you perform on your own device takes place on the basis of your consent and your legitimate interest in using the app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (f)). IP logging by Google Fonts/flagcdn.com is governed by those providers' policies.

6. Data Retention

We store none of your data on servers, so there is no retention period on our side. Local data on your device persists as long as you keep it and is deleted when you delete it.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, and erase your data directly on your device (simply clear the app data). For questions or data-subject requests (access, erasure, objection, portability) under the GDPR/PDP Law, contact us at support@faishal.id — we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page together with the revision date. This version is effective as of 2026-10-04.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@faishal.id.
