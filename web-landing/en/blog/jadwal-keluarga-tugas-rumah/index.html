Family Schedule and Home Chores

By Faishal · 2026-10-04 · 6 min read

Build a family schedule by listing every home chore, assigning them by each member's age and free time, then setting recurring reminders. petgo marks chores done so nothing is missed or doubled.

Listing every home chore

Household chaos usually starts with chores that have no clear owner. Write down every job, from sweeping to paying bills, then name one person responsible for each.

FAQ

What if a member refuses chores?

Start with light chores and offer choices; parental consistency spreads to kids.

How often to rotate?

Monthly is enough; too often confuses, too long bores.

Can small kids join?

Yes. Young children can tidy toys and fold blankets.

Does scheduling need internet?

No. petgo schedules and reminders run offline.
